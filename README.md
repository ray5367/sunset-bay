Sunset Bay is an original open-world crime game for the browser: explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal vehicles, dodge the police as your wanted level rises, and run missions for the city's crews. Every name, character, story beat, sound and line of art is original. It runs entirely offline from static files — no network request, no build step.

## What's in the city

- A 3.6 km coastal city: 8×8 road grid, beach and marina, docks, parks, hospital, bank, casino, diner, gun shop, customs garage and safehouse, with a day/night cycle and golden hour.
- 9 drivable vehicle types, live traffic that stops at lights, 46 pedestrians, parked cars to steal, and car damage, fires and explosions.
- A 5-star wanted system with pursuing cruisers, on-foot officers, a police helicopter at 5 stars, bust and evade mechanics.
- 6 story missions for Mara Quinn and Priya Nair against Victor Kane, plus taxi fares, the Sunset GP checkpoint race, 10 hidden packages, shops, gambling and a full save system.
- 5 weapons, combat on foot and drive-by pursuit, synthesized sound effects and music (no audio files), HUD with minimap, fullscreen map, and pause menu.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## Controls

WASD / arrows — move and drive · E — enter car, talk, shop · Mouse — aim · Click — shoot · F — punch · R — reload · 1–5 — weapons · M — map · P — pause · H — horn · Shift — run
